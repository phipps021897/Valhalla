Our Story

Forged In Weymouth, Built On Family

Valhalla Martial Arts was founded in 2018 with a vision to create the best facilities possible, with the best coaches possible. What started as one man's ambition has grown into a martial arts family — a welcoming, inclusive gym where beginners, competitors and everyone in between train side by side.

🥊

Expert Coaching

Our team of highly qualified, experienced coaches is dedicated to personalised guidance, backed by a genuine passion for martial arts.

🛡️

Inclusive Community

A welcoming, supportive environment where every member — whatever their age or ability — becomes part of the Valhalla family.

🪓

Diverse Training

Specialising in Muay Thai, with dedicated classes in K1 Kickboxing, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Wrestling and MMA.

🧠

Holistic Approach

Training builds more than fitness and self-defence — it develops mental resilience, discipline and personal growth.

🏆

Competitive Pathway

What We Train

Muay Thai, K1 Kickboxing & BJJ Classes In Weymouth

Muay Thai / K1 Kickboxing

Our flagship discipline — the art of eight limbs, from beginner fundamentals through to advanced fighter training.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Ground-fighting and submission grappling, run in partnership with Weymouth BJJ.

Wrestling / Glima

Takedowns, control and old-world grappling, blended into our MMA and open mat sessions.

MMA & Open Mat

Free-flow mixed martial arts practice for members to roll, spar and refine technique.

Kids & Teens Mixed Arts

Age-appropriate classes for ages 5–15, building confidence, fitness and discipline early.

Strength & Conditioning

Dedicated S&C sessions to build the power, endurance and resilience behind every fighter.
